## What proportion of remote listings now list AI as a required skill?

Our latest snapshot (2026‑10‑12) shows **487** remote jobs mentioning AI out of **3,928** analyzed listings, which translates to a **12%** share.

## Which remote roles are most likely to list AI requirements?

AI shows up across a surprisingly wide set of titles:

- **Senior Software Engineer – Healthcare / SaaS** (Sycle LLC) – AI‑enhanced diagnostics.
- **Sr. Principal Product Engineer – Cloud & Hyperscale** (Jabil) – AI workloads on massive infrastructure.
- **People Ops Partner** (Sardine) – AI‑driven talent analytics (salary quoted in CAD).
- **Business Development Representative** (Creator) – AI‑enabled SaaS sales pitches.
- **Insurance Agent** (HSS Careers) – AI‑assisted risk assessment (pay undisclosed).

Even non‑technical roles like **Live Ops Manager** at Devoted Studios list AI as a “nice‑to‑have” for automation.

## How does AI skill demand compare with other hot skills?

| Skill | Listings requiring it | Share of analysed listings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI | 487 | **12%** |
| Communication | 1,592 | **41%** |
| Leadership | 413 | **11%** |
| Sales | 356 | **9%** |
| Project Management | 305 | **8%** |
| Data Analysis | 267 | **7%** |

AI sits behind communication but ahead of leadership and sales in pure frequency, underscoring its growing relevance.

## What compensation signals are we seeing for AI‑related remote roles?

Only **22%** of all active remote listings disclose pay (844 of 3,919). Among those that do, the highest disclosed range is the **USD 132,400–238,400** yearly bracket at Jabil.

Other listings:

- **People Ops Partner – Sardine:** CAD 120,000–150,000 per year (not USD).
- **Senior Software Engineer – Sycle LLC:** CAD 120,000–140,000 per year.
- **Senior BDR – Creator:** CAD 62,000–75,000 per year.

The majority (78%) hide salary, so you’ll often need to negotiate based on market data rather than posted figures.

## Should you double‑down on AI skills if you’re already strong in your core discipline?

**Contrarian take:** While AI is a differentiator, the premium isn’t guaranteed. Companies still prioritize communication (41% of listings) and domain expertise.

## How can you showcase AI expertise in a remote application?

1. **Add a dedicated “AI Skills” section** on your resume (list frameworks, libraries, and projects).
2. **Link to a public repo or demo** (GitHub, Hugging Face).
3. **Mention AI in your cover letter** with concrete outcomes relevant to the role.
4. **Highlight cross‑functional AI work** (e.g., AI‑driven product decisions for non‑technical teams).

## What does the broader market say about AI‑focused services?

Industry analysts note the surge in AI‑driven cloud services. EY was named a **Leader in IDC MarketScape for WW Cloud Professional Services in the AI Era 2026**【 [https://www.ey.com/en\_gl/newsroom/2026/10/ey-named-a-leader-in-idc-marketscape-for-ww-cloud-professional-services-in-the-ai-era-2026】](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/newsroom/2026/10/ey-named-a-leader-in-idc-marketscape-for-ww-cloud-professional-services-in-the-ai-era-2026%E3%80%91), reinforcing that enterprises are investing heavily in AI capabilities.

## Methodology

- **Data source:** Loker Dollar remote‑job listings database (D1 jobs table).
- **Query date:** 2026‑10‑13.
- **Sample size:** 3,919 active listings; 3,928 listings used for skill‑demand snapshot.
- **Limitations:** Only listings present on the platform are captured; pay figures are self‑reported by employers; “AI” keyword may miss jobs that require AI without naming it.

- **Geographic salary differentials:** We lack breakdowns by country or region.
- **Skill depth:** Whether “AI” refers to basic prompt engineering or advanced deep‑learning research is unclear.
- **Hiring velocity:** Time‑to‑fill for AI‑required roles isn’t tracked in the current dataset.

## FAQ

### Which remote roles most often list AI as a requirement?

Roles ranging from senior engineering (e.g., Jabil’s Sr. Principal Product Engineer) to people operations (Sardine) and sales (Creator) now frequently mention AI.

### Does a disclosed salary guarantee a higher pay than undisclosed listings?

Not necessarily. Only **22%** of listings disclose pay, and the disclosed ranges vary widely. Many high‑pay roles still hide salary, so you’ll need to negotiate based on market benchmarks.

### How can I quickly verify if a remote job truly needs AI expertise?

Search the job description for specific AI terms (e.g., “machine learning,” “TensorFlow,” “prompt engineering”). If only “AI‑savvy” is mentioned, treat it as a soft requirement and be prepared to demonstrate concrete experience.
