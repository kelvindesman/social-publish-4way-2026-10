## What does the latest Loker Dollar data reveal about pay transparency?

Out of **4,441** active remote listings, only **1,000** (exactly **23%**) include a salary or hourly figure. The remaining **3,441** listings (**77%**) keep compensation private, a pattern that challenges the common belief that remote jobs are always upfront about pay.

Our skill‑demand snapshot examined **4,282** listings. The top‑required abilities are:

## How many remote jobs are actually open to worldwide applicants?

**2,753** of the **4,441** active listings (about **62%**) are tagged as “open to worldwide remote applicants.” That means roughly **two‑thirds** of the market truly supports a location‑agnostic work style.

## Are AI and leadership skills becoming prerequisites for remote work?

The data shows **517** listings (12%) require AI knowledge, while **416** listings (10%) look for leadership experience. Although these percentages are still modest, they are the fastest‑growing skill categories compared with other requirements, suggesting remote employers are beginning to value advanced technical and managerial capabilities.

## Methodology

- **Source:** Loker Dollar remote‑job listings database (query date 2026‑10‑14).
- **Sample size:** 4,441 active listings; 4,282 listings included in the skill‑demand analysis.
- **Filters:** Listings marked as remote; data on pay, location openness, and required skills extracted directly from the platform.
- **Limitations:** Pay figures are self‑reported by employers; 77% of listings omit compensation, so salary insights are incomplete. Skill tags rely on employer‑provided keywords, which may under‑represent informal requirements.
